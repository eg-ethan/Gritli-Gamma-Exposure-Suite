$$\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}(T) \= \\sqrt{\\frac{1}{T} \\sum\_{k=1}^T E\_t\\left\[\\sigma\_{t+k}^2\\right\]}$$

## **2\. Mathematical Formulations & Analytical Solvers**

### **A. GARCH-Driven American Greeks Engine (Bjerksund-Stensland 2002\)**

American exercise boundaries depend on cash carry $b \= r \- q$ and physical forward variance $\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2$. When $b \\ge r$, early call exercise is sub-optimal and the model reduces to the continuous terminal boundary. When $b \< r$, the optimal exercise boundary $I$ is:

$$\\beta \= \\left( \\frac{1}{2} \- \\frac{b}{\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2} \\right) \+ \\sqrt{\\left( \\frac{b}{\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2} \- \\frac{1}{2} \\right)^2 \+ \\frac{2r}{\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2}}$$

$$B\_\\infty \= \\frac{\\beta}{\\beta \- 1} K, \\quad B\_0 \= \\max\\left(K, \\frac{r}{r \- b} K\\right)$$

$$h \= \-(b T \+ 2\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}} \\sqrt{T}) \\left(\\frac{B\_0}{B\_\\infty \- B\_0}\\right), \\quad I \= B\_0 \+ (B\_\\infty \- B\_0)(1 \- e^h)$$  
If $S \\ge I$, call value is $S \- K$. Otherwise:

$$V\_{\\text{call}} \= \\alpha S^\\beta \- \\alpha \\phi(S, T, \\beta, I, I) \+ \\phi(S, T, 1, I, I) \- \\phi(S, T, 1, K, I) \- K \\phi(S, T, 0, I, I) \+ K \\phi(S, T, 0, K, I)$$  
Where $\\alpha \= (I \- K) I^{-\\beta}$. American puts are priced via the symmetry transform:

$$V\_{\\text{put}}(S, K, T, r, q, \\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}) \= V\_{\\text{call}}(K, S, T, q, r, \\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}})$$  
Greeks are computed via central finite differencing directly on the GARCH pricing surface:

* $\\Delta \= \\frac{V(S \+ h\_S) \- V(S \- h\_S)}{2 h\_S}$

* $\\Gamma \= \\frac{V(S \+ h\_S) \- 2V(S) \+ V(S \- h\_S)}{h\_S^2}$

* $\\text{Vanna} \= \\frac{\\Delta(\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}} \+ h\_\\sigma) \- \\Delta(\\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}} \- h\_\\sigma)}{2 h\_\\sigma}$

* $\\text{Charm} \= \-\\frac{\\Delta(T) \- \\Delta(T \- h\_T)}{h\_T}$

### **B. GARCH-Anchored Gatheral Quasi-SVI Surface**

Total implied variance $w(k, T)$ across log-moneyness $k \= \\ln(K / F\_t)$ is anchored to the forward GARCH variance $w\_0 \= \\bar{\\sigma}\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2 T$:

$$w(k; a, b, \\rho, m, \\sigma) \= a \+ b \\left\[ \\rho (k \- m) \+ \\sqrt{(k \- m)^2 \+ \\sigma^2} \\right\]$$  
Subject to Gatheral-Jacquier no-arbitrage constraints:

> 1. $b \\ge 0$  
> 2. $\\vert{}\\rho\\vert{} \< 1$  
> 3. $\\sigma \> 0$  
> 4. $a \+ b \\sigma \\sqrt{1 \- \\rho^2} \\ge 0$  
> 5. $b(1 \+ \\vert{}\\rho\\vert{}) \< \\frac{4}{T}$

### **C. GARCH-Scaled Dynamic Aggressor Classification (BVC & Lee-Ready)**

Trade direction $D\_k$ on individual ticks uses the Lee-Ready (1991) logic:

$$D\_k \= \\begin{cases} \+1 & \\text{if } P\_k \> M\_k \\\\ \-1 & \\text{if } P\_k \< M\_k \\\\ D\_{k-1} & \\text{if } P\_k \= M\_k \\text{ and } P\_k \\neq P\_{k-1} \\end{cases}$$  
Aggregated 0DTE flow bars are signed via Bulk Volume Classification normalized by the instantaneous GARCH conditional volatility $\\sigma\_{\\tau,\\text{GARCH}}$:

$$V\_\\tau^{\\text{buy}} \= V\_\\tau \\cdot \\Phi\\left(\\frac{P\_\\tau \- P\_{\\tau-1}}{\\sigma\_{\\tau,\\text{GARCH}}}\\right), \\quad V\_\\tau^{\\text{sell}} \= V\_\\tau \- V\_\\tau^{\\text{buy}}$$

### **D. Dollar Dealer Exposures & Structural Boundaries**

* **Dollar Gamma Exposure (GEX):** Dollar value of underlying shares required to rehedge per 1% move in spot\[cite: 1, 4\]:  
  $$\\text{GEX}\_i \= q\_{i,t} \\cdot \\Gamma\_i \\cdot S^2 \\cdot 0.01 \\cdot M \\quad (M \= 100\) \\text{\[cite: 1, 4\]}$$  
* **Dollar Delta Exposure (DEX):** Net directional exposure held by dealers:  
  $$\\text{DEX}\_i \= q\_{i,t} \\cdot \\Delta\_i \\cdot S \\cdot M \\text{\[cite: 1, 2\]}$$  
* **Dollar Vanna Exposure (VEX):** Rehedging flow triggered per 1% absolute shift in volatility:  
  $$\\text{VEX}\_i \= q\_{i,t} \\cdot \\text{Vanna}\_i \\cdot S \\cdot 0.01 \\cdot M \\text{\[cite: 1, 2\]}$$  
* **Dollar Charm Exposure (CHEX):** Mechanical daily cash-flow obligation driven by time decay\[cite: 1, 2\]:  
  $$\\text{CHEX}\_i \= q\_{i,t} \\cdot \\text{Charm}\_i \\cdot S \\cdot M \\text{\[cite: 1, 2\]}$$  
* **Zero-Gamma Flip ($S^\*$):** The spot level where aggregate dealer exposure transitions between volatility dampening and acceleration:  
  $$\\sum\_{i=1}^N \\text{GEX}\_i(S^\*) \= 0 \\text{\[cite: 1, 5\]}$$  
* **Call Wall & Put Wall:** Major structural resistance and support levels\[cite: 1, 5\]:  
  $$K\_{\\text{CallWall}} \= \\arg\\max\_K \\sum\_{i \\in \\text{Calls}(K)} \\text{GEX}\_i, \\quad K\_{\\text{PutWall}} \= \\arg\\min\_K \\sum\_{i \\in \\text{Puts}(K)} \\text{GEX}\_i \\text{\[cite: 1\]}$$

### **E. Microstructure Execution & Friction Mechanics**

* **GARCH-Scaled Whalley-Wilmott Hedging Band:** Incorporates GARCH physical variance $\\sigma\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2$ to govern discrete rebalancing:  
  $$\\text{Band}(S, t) \= \\left\[ \\Delta\_t \- \\left(\\frac{3 \\lambda S\_t \\Gamma\_t^2}{2 \\gamma\_A \\sigma\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2}\\right)^{1/3}, \\quad \\Delta\_t \+ \\left(\\frac{3 \\lambda S\_t \\Gamma\_t^2}{2 \\gamma\_A \\sigma\_{\\text{GARCH}}^2}\\right)^{1/3} \\right\]$$  
* **Avellaneda-Lipkin Expiration Strike Pinning:** Quantifies the mean-reverting gravitational pull toward strike $K$\[cite: 3, 4\]:  
  $$dS\_t \= \-\\kappa(S\_t \- K) dt \+ \\sigma\_{\\text{GARCH}} dW\_t, \\quad \\kappa \= \\frac{\\text{GEX}(K)}{\\text{Depth}\_{\\text{cash}}} \\text{\[cite: 3\]}$$  
* **Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) & Kyle's Lambda:**  
  $$\\text{OFI}\_t \= I\_t \\Delta Q\_{b,t} \- (1 \- I\_t) \\Delta Q\_{a,t}$$  
  $$\\Delta S\_t \= \\lambda\_{\\text{Kyle}} \\left( \\text{HedgeFlow}\_t \+ \\text{OFI}\_t \\right) \+ \\epsilon\_t, \\quad \\lambda\_{\\text{Kyle}} \= \\frac{\\text{Cov}(\\Delta S\_t, \\text{NetVolume}\_t)}{\\text{Var}(\\text{NetVolume}\_t)}$$

    // 2\. GARCH-DRIVEN AMERICAN GREEKS SOLVER (BJERKSUND-STENSLAND 2002\)  
    // \=========================================================================

    public static class GarchAmericanSolver  
    {  
        private static double NormalCdf(double x)  
        {  
            return 0.5 \* (1.0 \+ Math.Erf(x / Math.Sqrt(2.0)));  
        }

        private static double BjerksundStenslandCall(double S, double K, double T, double r, double q, double sigmaGarch)  
        {  
            if (T \<= 0.0) return Math.Max(0.0, S \- K);

            double b \= r \- q;  
            double v2 \= sigmaGarch \* sigmaGarch;

            if (b \>= r)  
            {  
                double d1 \= (Math.Log(S / K) \+ (b \+ 0.5 \* v2) \* T) / (sigmaGarch \* Math.Sqrt(T));  
                double d2 \= d1 \- sigmaGarch \* Math.Sqrt(T);  
                return S \* Math.Exp(-q \* T) \* NormalCdf(d1) \- K \* Math.Exp(-r \* T) \* NormalCdf(d2);  
            }

            double beta \= (0.5 \- b / v2) \+ Math.Sqrt(Math.Pow(b / v2 \- 0.5, 2.0) \+ 2.0 \* r / v2);  
            double bInf \= (beta / (beta \- 1.0)) \* K;  
            double b0 \= Math.Max(K, (r / (r \- b)) \* K);

            double h \= \-(b \* T \+ 2.0 \* sigmaGarch \* Math.Sqrt(T)) \* (b0 / (bInf \- b0));  
            double triggerI \= b0 \+ (bInf \- b0) \* (1.0 \- Math.Exp(h));

            if (S \>= triggerI) return S \- K;

            double alpha \= (triggerI \- K) \* Math.Pow(triggerI, \-beta);

            double Phi(double sIn, double tIn, double gammaExp, double hBoundary, double iBoundary)  
            {  
                double lambdaVal \= (-r \+ gammaExp \* b \+ 0.5 \* gammaExp \* (gammaExp \- 1.0) \* v2) \* tIn;  
                double d \= \-(Math.Log(sIn / hBoundary) \+ (b \+ (gammaExp \- 0.5) \* v2) \* tIn) / (sigmaGarch \* Math.Sqrt(tIn));  
                double kappa \= (2.0 \* b) / v2 \+ (2.0 \* gammaExp \- 1.0);  
                return Math.Exp(lambdaVal) \* Math.Pow(sIn, gammaExp) \* (  
                    NormalCdf(d) \- Math.Pow(iBoundary / sIn, kappa) \* NormalCdf(  
                        d \- (2.0 \* Math.Log(iBoundary / sIn)) / (sigmaGarch \* Math.Sqrt(tIn))  
                    )  
                );  
            }

            return alpha \* Math.Pow(S, beta)  
                   \- alpha \* Phi(S, T, beta, triggerI, triggerI)  
                   \+ Phi(S, T, 1.0, triggerI, triggerI)  
                   \- Phi(S, T, 1.0, K, triggerI)  
                   \- K \* Phi(S, T, 0.0, triggerI, triggerI)  
                   \+ K \* Phi(S, T, 0.0, K, triggerI);  
        }

        public static OptionGreeks ComputeGreeks(double spot, double strike, double expiryYears,   
                                                 double r, double q, double sigmaGarch, char right)  
        {  
            double flag \= (char.ToUpperInvariant(right) \== 'C') ? 1.0 : \-1.0;

            double EvaluatePrice(double s, double t, double vol)  
            {  
                if (flag \> 0)  
                {  
                    return BjerksundStenslandCall(s, strike, t, r, q, vol);  
                }  
                // American Put-Call symmetry relation  
                return BjerksundStenslandCall(strike, s, t, r \- (r \- q), r, vol);  
            }

            double hs \= Math.Max(1e-4, spot \* 0.0001);  
            double ht \= 1.0 / 365.0;  
            double hvol \= 1e-4;

            double basePrice \= EvaluatePrice(spot, expiryYears, sigmaGarch);  
            double priceUp \= EvaluatePrice(spot \+ hs, expiryYears, sigmaGarch);  
            double priceDn \= EvaluatePrice(spot \- hs, expiryYears, sigmaGarch);

            double delta \= (priceUp \- priceDn) / (2.0 \* hs);  
            double gamma \= (priceUp \- 2.0 \* basePrice \+ priceDn) / (hs \* hs);

            // Vanna: dDelta / dVol  
            double pUpVolUp \= EvaluatePrice(spot \+ hs, expiryYears, sigmaGarch \+ hvol);  
            double pDnVolUp \= EvaluatePrice(spot \- hs, expiryYears, sigmaGarch \+ hvol);  
            double deltaVolUp \= (pUpVolUp \- pDnVolUp) / (2.0 \* hs);  
            double vanna \= (deltaVolUp \- delta) / hvol;

            // Charm: \-dDelta / dt  
            double tDecay \= Math.Max(1e-5, expiryYears \- ht);  
            double pUpT \= EvaluatePrice(spot \+ hs, tDecay, sigmaGarch);  
            double pDnT \= EvaluatePrice(spot \- hs, tDecay, sigmaGarch);  
            double deltaT \= (pUpT \- pDnT) / (2.0 \* hs);  
            double charm \= \-(deltaT \- delta) / ht;

            return new OptionGreeks(  
                Math.Max(0.0, basePrice),  
                delta,  
                gamma,  
                vanna,  
                charm  
            );  
        }  
    }

    // \=========================================================================  
    // 3\. GARCH-ANCHORED ARBITRAGE-FREE SVI SURFACE  
    // \=========================================================================

    public readonly record struct SviParameters(double A, double B, double Rho, double M, double Sigma);

    public static class GarchSviSurface  
    {  
        public static double CalculateTotalVariance(double logMoneyness, SviParameters p)  
        {  
            return p.A \+ p.B \* (p.Rho \* (logMoneyness \- p.M) \+   
                   Math.Sqrt(Math.Pow(logMoneyness \- p.M, 2.0) \+ p.Sigma \* p.Sigma));  
        }

        public static double GetImpliedVolatility(double strike, double forward, double expiryYears, SviParameters p)  
        {  
            if (expiryYears \<= 0.0) return 0.0;  
            double k \= Math.Log(strike / forward);  
            double totalVariance \= CalculateTotalVariance(k, p);  
            return Math.Sqrt(Math.Max(1e-6, totalVariance / expiryYears));  
        }

        public static bool ValidateArbitrageConstraints(SviParameters p, double expiryYears)  
        {  
            if (p.B \< 0.0) return false;  
            if (Math.Abs(p.Rho) \>= 1.0) return false;  
            if (p.Sigma \<= 0.0) return false;  
            if (p.A \+ p.B \* p.Sigma \* Math.Sqrt(Math.Max(0.0, 1.0 \- p.Rho \* p.Rho)) \< 0.0) return false;  
            if (p.B \* (1.0 \+ Math.Abs(p.Rho)) \>= 4.0 / Math.Max(expiryYears, 1e-4)) return false;  
            return true;  
        }  
    }

    public class MicrostructureClassifier  
    {  
        private double? \_lastPrice;  
        private int \_lastDirection;

        public int ClassifyLeeReady(double tradePrice, double bestBid, double bestAsk)  
        {  
            double midpoint \= (bestBid \+ bestAsk) / 2.0;  
            int direction;

            if (tradePrice \> midpoint)  
            {  
                direction \= 1;  
            }  
            else if (tradePrice \< midpoint)  
            {  
                direction \= \-1;  
            }  
            else  
            {  
                if (\_lastPrice.HasValue)  
                {  
                    if (tradePrice \> \_lastPrice.Value) direction \= 1;  
                    else if (tradePrice \< \_lastPrice.Value) direction \= \-1;  
                    else direction \= \_lastDirection;  
                }  
                else  
                {  
                    direction \= 0;  
                }  
            }

            \_lastPrice \= tradePrice;  
            \_lastDirection \= direction;  
            return direction;  
        }

        public static (double BuyVolume, double SellVolume, double NetFlow)   
            ClassifyBulkVolume(double deltaPrice, double totalVolume, double sigmaGarchIntra)  
        {  
            if (sigmaGarchIntra \<= 1e-8) return (totalVolume \* 0.5, totalVolume \* 0.5, 0.0);

            double zScore \= deltaPrice / sigmaGarchIntra;  
            double probBuy \= 0.5 \* (1.0 \+ Math.Erf(zScore / Math.Sqrt(2.0)));  
            double vBuy \= totalVolume \* probBuy;  
            double vSell \= totalVolume \* (1.0 \- probBuy);  
            return (vBuy, vSell, vBuy \- vSell);  
        }  
    }

    public class StatefulDealerInventory  
    {  
        private readonly ConcurrentDictionary\<string, double\> \_dealerInventories \= new();

        public void InitializeContract(string localSymbol, double baselineOi, char right, string mode \= "flow\_only")  
        {  
            double initialInventory \= 0.0;  
            if (mode.Equals("squeezemetrics", StringComparison.OrdinalIgnoreCase))  
            {  
                // Classical SqueezeMetrics baseline convention\[cite: 4\]  
                initialInventory \= (char.ToUpperInvariant(right) \== 'C') ? baselineOi : \-baselineOi;  
            }  
            \_dealerInventories.AddOrUpdate(localSymbol, initialInventory, (\_, \_) \=\> initialInventory);  
        }

        public void IngestTrade(string localSymbol, double tradePrice, double size,   
                                double bestBid, double bestAsk, MicrostructureClassifier classifier)  
        {  
            int customerDirection \= classifier.ClassifyLeeReady(tradePrice, bestBid, bestAsk);  
            // Counterparty invariant: Dealers take the other side\[cite: 3, 4\]  
            double dealerDirection \= \-customerDirection;  
            \_dealerInventories.AddOrUpdate(localSymbol, dealerDirection \* size, (\_, current) \=\> current \+ dealerDirection \* size);  
        }

        public double GetPosition(string localSymbol)  
        {  
            return \_dealerInventories.TryGetValue(localSymbol, out double q) ? q : 0.0;  
        }  
    }

    public class AggregateRiskEngine  
    {  
        private readonly int \_contractMultiplier;

        public AggregateRiskEngine(int contractMultiplier \= 100)  
        {  
            \_contractMultiplier \= contractMultiplier;  
        }

        public ContractExposure EvaluateContract(OptionContractKey key, double dealerInventory,   
                                                 double spot, double r, double q, double sigmaGarch)  
        {  
            OptionGreeks greeks \= GarchAmericanSolver.ComputeGreeks(  
                spot, key.Strike, key.ExpiryYears, r, q, sigmaGarch, key.Right  
            );

            double dollarGex \= dealerInventory \* greeks.Gamma \* (spot \* spot) \* 0.01 \* \_contractMultiplier; //\[cite: 1, 4\]  
            double dollarDex \= dealerInventory \* greeks.Delta \* spot \* \_contractMultiplier; //\[cite: 1, 2\]  
            double dollarVex \= dealerInventory \* greeks.Vanna \* spot \* 0.01 \* \_contractMultiplier; //\[cite: 1, 2\]  
            double dollarChex \= dealerInventory \* greeks.Charm \* spot \* \_contractMultiplier; //\[cite: 1, 2\]

            return new ContractExposure(key, dealerInventory, dollarGex, dollarDex, dollarVex, dollarChex);  
        }

        public (double CallWall, double PutWall) FindWalls(IEnumerable\<ContractExposure\> exposures)  
        {  
            var expList \= exposures.ToList();

            var callWall \= expList  
                .Where(e \=\> char.ToUpperInvariant(e.Key.Right) \== 'C')  
                .GroupBy(e \=\> e.Key.Strike)  
                .Select(g \=\> new { Strike \= g.Key, NetGex \= g.Sum(x \=\> x.DollarGex) })  
                .OrderByDescending(x \=\> x.NetGex)  
                .Select(x \=\> (double?)x.Strike)  
                .FirstOrDefault() ?? double.NaN; //\[cite: 1\]

            var putWall \= expList  
                .Where(e \=\> char.ToUpperInvariant(e.Key.Right) \== 'P')  
                .GroupBy(e \=\> e.Key.Strike)  
                .Select(g \=\> new { Strike \= g.Key, NetGex \= g.Sum(x \=\> x.DollarGex) })  
                .OrderBy(x \=\> x.NetGex)  
                .Select(x \=\> (double?)x.Strike)  
                .FirstOrDefault() ?? double.NaN; //\[cite: 1\]

            return (callWall, putWall);  
        }

        public double SolveGammaFlip(IReadOnlyList\<OptionContractKey\> keys,   
                                      StatefulDealerInventory inventory,   
                                      double currentSpot, double r, double q,   
                                      double sigmaGarch)  
        {  
            double EvaluateTotalGex(double hypotheticalSpot)  
            {  
                double total \= 0.0;  
                for (int i \= 0; i \< keys.Count; i++)  
                {  
                    var k \= keys\[i\];  
                    double qInv \= inventory.GetPosition(k.LocalSymbol);  
                    OptionGreeks g \= GarchAmericanSolver.ComputeGreeks(  
                        hypotheticalSpot, k.Strike, k.ExpiryYears, r, q, sigmaGarch, k.Right  
                    );  
                    total \+= qInv \* g.Gamma \* (hypotheticalSpot \* hypotheticalSpot) \* 0.01 \* \_contractMultiplier;  
                }  
                return total;  
            }

            double lower \= currentSpot \* 0.80;  
            double upper \= currentSpot \* 1.20;

            return BrentRootFinder.FindRoot(EvaluateTotalGex, lower, upper, 1e-3, 100); //\[cite: 1, 5\]  
        }  
    }

    public static class BrentRootFinder  
    {  
        public static double FindRoot(Func\<double, double\> f, double a, double b, double tol \= 1e-3, int maxIter \= 100)  
        {  
            double fa \= f(a);  
            double fb \= f(b);

            if (fa \* fb \> 0.0) return double.NaN; // Must bracket zero\[cite: 1, 5\]

            if (Math.Abs(fa) \< Math.Abs(fb))  
            {  
                (a, b) \= (b, a);  
                (fa, fb) \= (fb, fa);  
            }

            double c \= a;  
            double fc \= fa;  
            bool mflag \= true;  
            double d \= 0.0;

            for (int iter \= 0; iter \< maxIter; iter++)  
            {  
                double s;  
                if (Math.Abs(fa \- fc) \> 1e-12 && Math.Abs(fb \- fc) \> 1e-12)  
                {  
                    // Inverse quadratic interpolation  
                    s \= (a \* fb \* fc) / ((fa \- fb) \* (fa \- fc)) \+  
                        (b \* fa \* fc) / ((fb \- fa) \* (fb \- fc)) \+  
                        (c \* fa \* fb) / ((fc \- fa) \* (fc \- fb));  
                }  
                else  
                {  
                    // Secant method  
                    s \= b \- fb \* (b \- a) / (fb \- fa);  
                }

                double condition1 \= (s \< (3.0 \* a \+ b) / 4.0 && s \> b) || (s \> (3.0 \* a \+ b) / 4.0 && s \< b);  
                double condition2 \= mflag && Math.Abs(s \- b) \>= Math.Abs(b \- c) / 2.0;  
                double condition3 \= \!mflag && Math.Abs(s \- b) \>= Math.Abs(c \- d) / 2.0;  
                double condition4 \= mflag && Math.Abs(b \- c) \< tol;  
                double condition5 \= \!mflag && Math.Abs(c \- d) \< tol;

                if (condition1 || condition2 || condition3 || condition4 || condition5)  
                {  
                    // Bisection fallback  
                    s \= (a \+ b) / 2.0;  
                    mflag \= true;  
                }  
                else  
                {  
                    mflag \= false;  
                }

                double fs \= f(s);  
                d \= c;  
                c \= b;  
                fc \= fb;

                if (fa \* fs \< 0.0)  
                {  
                    b \= s;  
                    fb \= fs;  
                }  
                else  
                {  
                    a \= s;  
                    fa \= fs;  
                }

                if (Math.Abs(fa) \< Math.Abs(fb))  
                {  
                    (a, b) \= (b, a);  
                    (fa, fb) \= (fb, fa);  
                }

                if (Math.Abs(b \- a) \< tol || Math.Abs(fb) \< 1e-9) return b;  
            }

            return b;  
        }  
    }

    public static class MicrostructureExecutionMechanics  
    {  
        public static (double LowerDelta, double UpperDelta, double BandWidth)   
            CalculateWhalleyWilmottBand(double delta, double gamma, double spot,   
                                        double sigmaGarch, double lambdaCost \= 0.0005, double gammaA \= 1e-6)  
        {  
            double garchVar \= Math.Max(1e-6, sigmaGarch \* sigmaGarch);  
            double denom \= 2.0 \* gammaA \* garchVar;  
            double width \= Math.Pow((3.0 \* lambdaCost \* spot \* (gamma \* gamma)) / denom, 1.0 / 3.0);  
            return (delta \- width, delta \+ width, width);  
        }

        public static (double DriftPull, double Kappa, bool IsStabilizing)   
            CalculateAvellanedaPinning(double spot, double strike, double strikeGex, double cashDepthDollars)  
        {  
            if (cashDepthDollars \<= 0.0) return (0.0, 0.0, false);  
            double kappa \= strikeGex / cashDepthDollars; //  
            double drift \= \-kappa \* (spot \- strike);  
            return (drift, kappa, strikeGex \> 0.0); // Positive GEX pins; Negative GEX repels\[cite: 1, 3\]  
        }

        public static double CalculateOFI(IReadOnlyList\<double\> bids, IReadOnlyList\<double\> bidSizes,  
                                          IReadOnlyList\<double\> asks, IReadOnlyList\<double\> askSizes)  
        {  
            if (bids.Count \< 2) return 0.0;  
            double ofi \= 0.0;

            for (int i \= 1; i \< bids.Count; i++)  
            {  
                double deltaQb \= (bids\[i\] \> bids\[i \- 1\]) ? bidSizes\[i\] :  
                                 (bids\[i\] \== bids\[i \- 1\]) ? bidSizes\[i\] \- bidSizes\[i \- 1\] : 0.0;

                double deltaQa \= (asks\[i\] \< asks\[i \- 1\]) ? askSizes\[i\] :  
                                 (asks\[i\] \== asks\[i \- 1\]) ? askSizes\[i\] \- askSizes\[i \- 1\] : 0.0;

                ofi \+= (deltaQb \- deltaQa);  
            }

            return ofi;  
        }

        public static double ComputeKylesLambda(ReadOnlySpan\<double\> priceDeltas, ReadOnlySpan\<double\> netOrderFlow)  
        {  
            if (priceDeltas.Length \!= netOrderFlow.Length || priceDeltas.Length \< 2) return 0.0;

            double meanP \= 0.0, meanV \= 0.0;  
            for (int i \= 0; i \< priceDeltas.Length; i++)  
            {  
                meanP \+= priceDeltas\[i\];  
                meanV \+= netOrderFlow\[i\];  
            }  
            meanP /= priceDeltas.Length;  
            meanV /= priceDeltas.Length;

            double covariance \= 0.0, varianceV \= 0.0;  
            for (int i \= 0; i \< priceDeltas.Length; i++)  
            {  
                double diffP \= priceDeltas\[i\] \- meanP;  
                double diffV \= netOrderFlow\[i\] \- meanV;  
                covariance \+= diffP \* diffV;  
                varianceV \+= diffV \* diffV;  
            }

            return (varianceV \> 1e-12) ? covariance / varianceV : 0.0;  
        }  
    }

    public class DealerExposurePipeline  
    {  
        private readonly AggregateRiskEngine \_riskEngine;  
        private readonly StatefulDealerInventory \_inventory;  
        private readonly MicrostructureClassifier \_classifier;  
        private readonly List\<OptionContractKey\> \_activeContracts \= new();

        public double CurrentSpot { get; private set; }

        public DealerExposurePipeline(int contractMultiplier \= 100)  
        {  
            \_riskEngine \= new AggregateRiskEngine(contractMultiplier);  
            \_inventory \= new StatefulDealerInventory();  
            \_classifier \= new MicrostructureClassifier();  
        }

        public void RegisterContract(string localSymbol, double strike, char right, double expiryYears, double baselineOi)  
        {  
            var key \= new OptionContractKey(localSymbol, strike, right, expiryYears);  
            \_activeContracts.Add(key);  
            \_inventory.InitializeContract(localSymbol, baselineOi, right, mode: "flow\_only");  
        }

        public void OnEquityTick(double price)  
        {  
            CurrentSpot \= price;  
        }

        public void OnOptionTrade(string localSymbol, double tradePrice, double size, double bid, double ask)  
        {  
            \_inventory.IngestTrade(localSymbol, tradePrice, size, bid, ask, \_classifier);  
        }

        public SystemExposureSnapshot GenerateSnapshot(double r, double q, double sigmaGarch)  
        {  
            if (CurrentSpot \<= 0.0 || \_activeContracts.Count \== 0)  
            {  
                return default;  
            }

            var exposures \= new List\<ContractExposure\>(\_activeContracts.Count);  
            double totalGex \= 0.0;  
            double totalDex \= 0.0;  
            double totalVex \= 0.0;  
            double totalChex \= 0.0;

            for (int i \= 0; i \< \_activeContracts.Count; i++)  
            {  
                var key \= \_activeContracts\[i\];  
                double qPos \= \_inventory.GetPosition(key.LocalSymbol);  
                ContractExposure exp \= \_riskEngine.EvaluateContract(key, qPos, CurrentSpot, r, q, sigmaGarch);

                exposures.Add(exp);  
                totalGex \+= exp.DollarGex;  
                totalDex \+= exp.DollarDex;  
                totalVex \+= exp.DollarVex;  
                totalChex \+= exp.DollarChex;  
            }

            var (callWall, putWall) \= \_riskEngine.FindWalls(exposures);  
            double gammaFlip \= \_riskEngine.SolveGammaFlip(\_activeContracts, \_inventory, CurrentSpot, r, q, sigmaGarch);

            string regime \= (totalGex \> 0.0)   
                ? "POSITIVE\_GAMMA (Mean-Reverting Cushion / Suppressed Realized Volatility)"  //  
                : "NEGATIVE\_GAMMA (Pro-Cyclical Acceleration / Elevated Volatility Breakout)"; //\[cite: 1, 3, 5\]

            return new SystemExposureSnapshot(  
                CurrentSpot,  
                totalGex,  
                totalDex,  
                totalVex,  
                totalChex,  
                callWall,  
                putWall,  
                gammaFlip,  
                regime  
            );  
        }  
    }

## **4\. Verification & Operational Directives**

### **A. Non-Zero Solution Guarantees**

> 1. **Finite Difference Boundaries ($h\_S, h\_\\sigma, h\_T$):** The step parameter $h\_S \= \\max(10^{-4}, S \\cdot 0.0001)$ scales dynamically with share price to eliminate floating-point truncation when evaluating low-priced or split-adjusted equities\[cite: 3\].  
> 2. **Early Exercise Check:** When underlying cash carry is negative ($b \< 0$), early exercise on deep ITM American puts occurs. The analytical check if (S \>= triggerI) return S \- K; protects finite differencing steps against invalid branch values\[cite: 2\].

### **B. Regime Threshold Rules**

* **Spot vs. Call Wall:** When spot trades beneath a concentrated positive Call Wall, dealer delta hedging provides resistance on tests into the wall (dealers short delta against customer call sells, liquidating stock on approaches)\[cite: 1, 5\].  
* **Spot vs. Zero-Gamma Line:** Crossing below $S^\*$ shifts total portfolio hedging from mean-reverting (selling highs, buying lows) into pro-cyclical acceleration (selling into breakdowns, chasing upside rallies)\[cite: 1, 3, 5\].  
* **GARCH Variance Narrowing:** As GARCH conditional standard deviation $\\sigma\_{\\tau,\\text{GARCH}}$ expands during intraday volatility spikes, the Whalley-Wilmott hedge band width narrows:

$$\\text{BandWidth} \\propto \\sigma\_{\\text{GARCH}}^{-2/3}$$  
This forces dealer hedge transactions to hit the market in tighter discrete intervals rather than buffering order flow\[cite: 3, 4\].
